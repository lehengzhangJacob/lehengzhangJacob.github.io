GRCF addresses limitations of pointwise regression and pairwise ordinal learning in multimodal sentiment analysis. Inspired by Group Relative Policy Optimization, it uses a two-stage framework that preserves relative ordinal structure while keeping absolute sentiment scores calibrated.

- Preserves relative ordinal structure while calibrating absolute sentiment scores.
- Uses GRPO-inspired advantage-weighted ranking with dynamic margins.
- Accepted at ACM Multimedia 2026 (second author).
