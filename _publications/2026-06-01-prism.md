PRISM proposes a primary-modality-driven dynamic fusion framework combining variational token bottlenecks, sample-level differentiable primary-modality routing, and confidence-aware gating.

- Selects a primary modality dynamically instead of relying on fixed fusion rules.
- Suppresses noisy modality features with information bottleneck mechanisms.
- Uses confidence-aware gating for trainable auxiliary modality selection.
