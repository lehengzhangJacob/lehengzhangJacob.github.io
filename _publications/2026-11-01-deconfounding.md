This paper proposes CaMIB, which disentangles causal and confounding representations through information bottlenecks, causal intervention, and instrumental-variable constraints to improve OOD generalization under distribution shifts.

- Purifies unimodal inputs through variational information bottlenecks.
- Splits fused representations into causal and confounding branches via a mask generator.
- Evaluated on sentiment analysis, humor detection, sarcasm detection, and OOD test sets.
