GRCF: Group-wise Ranking and Calibration Framework for Multimodal Sentiment Analysis

Manning Gao, Leheng Zhang, Shiqin Han, Haifeng Hu, Yuncheng Jiang, and Sijie Mai. arXiv:2601.09606.

PDF arXiv

Inspired by group-relative advantage weighting in GRPO, GRCF introduces adaptive hard-sample weighting, dynamic margins, and two-stage ranking calibration for multimodal sentiment analysis.

  • Preserves relative ordinal structure while calibrating absolute sentiment scores.
  • Uses GRPO-inspired advantage-weighted ranking with dynamic margins.
  • Accepted at ACM Multimedia 2026 (second author).
Interspeech 2026 · Accepted (Oral) · 3rd Author

Uncovering Latent Depression Severity for Binary Depression Detection via Advantage-weighting Ranking

Manning Gao, Tingyi Liu, Leheng Zhang, Haifeng Hu, Yuncheng Jiang, and Sijie Mai.

PDF

This work introduces the BAR loss to recover the latent ordinal structure of depression severity and improve hard-sample discrimination for binary depression detection.

  • Uses a temporal encoder and mutual transformer for deep cross-modal fusion.
  • Reconstructs latent ordinal structure by prioritizing hard pairs.
  • Accepted at Interspeech 2026 as an Oral presentation (third author).

Information Fusion · Under Review · First Author

PRISM: Primary-Centric Multimodal Fusion with Information Bottlenecks and Confidence-Aware Gating

First-author submission to Information Fusion.

PDF

PRISM proposes a primary-modality-driven dynamic fusion framework combining variational token bottlenecks, sample-level differentiable primary-modality routing, and confidence-aware gating.

  • Selects a primary modality dynamically instead of relying on fixed fusion rules.
  • Suppresses noisy modality features with information bottleneck mechanisms.
  • Uses confidence-aware gating for trainable auxiliary modality selection.
EMNLP 2026 · Under Review · First Author

RITHM: Recursive Information-Theoretic Halting for Multimodal Sentiment Understanding

First-author submission to EMNLP 2026.

PDF

RITHM combines recursive information bottlenecks, information-gain-based halting, and syntax-aware composition for sample-adaptive multimodal reasoning.

  • Distills task-relevant acoustic and visual evidence into compact latent representations.
  • Updates bottleneck states recursively according to sample difficulty.
  • Evaluated on multimodal sentiment analysis and multimodal humor detection tasks.
AAAI 2027 · Under Review · 2nd Author

Deconfounding Multimodal Affective Computing via Minimal Global Causal Representations

AAAI 2027 submission (second author).

PDF

This paper proposes CaMIB, which disentangles causal and confounding representations through information bottlenecks, causal intervention, and instrumental-variable constraints to improve OOD generalization under distribution shifts.

  • Purifies unimodal inputs through variational information bottlenecks.
  • Splits fused representations into causal and confounding branches via a mask generator.
  • Evaluated on sentiment analysis, humor detection, sarcasm detection, and OOD test sets.
