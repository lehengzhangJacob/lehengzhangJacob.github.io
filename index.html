About Me

华南师范大学计算机科学与技术专业本科生（大四）。研究兴趣为多模态计算、具身智能与 AI for Science。目前在麦思杰教授指导下开展多模态情感计算研究，并与中山大学肿瘤防治中心曾木圣院士团队合作开展 EB 病毒靶向药物相关工作。计划继续攻读博士学位。

English Version. I am a senior undergraduate student in Computer Science and Technology at South China Normal University. My research interests are multimodal computing, embodied intelligence, and AI for Science. I work with Prof. Sijie Mai on multimodal affective computing, and I have collaborated with Academician Musheng Zeng's team at Sun Yat-sen University Cancer Center on targeted drug research for Epstein–Barr virus. I plan to pursue doctoral studies in artificial intelligence.
