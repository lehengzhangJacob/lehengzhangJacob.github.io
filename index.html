English Version. I am a senior undergraduate student in Computer Science and Technology at South China Normal University (GPA 4.02/5.0, major ranking 1/160). I work with Prof. Sijie Mai on multimodal affective computing and multimodal large language models, and I have collaborated with Academician Musheng Zeng’s team at Sun Yat-sen University Cancer Center on targeted drug research for Epstein–Barr virus. I have papers accepted at ACM Multimedia 2026 and Interspeech 2026 (Oral), with first-author manuscripts under review. I plan to pursue a Ph.D. in artificial intelligence.
